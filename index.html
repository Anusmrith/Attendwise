Install on iPhone / iPad

Add AttendWise to your iOS home screen as a standalone application:

  1. 1 Tap the Share icon (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom toolbar of Safari.
  2. 2 Scroll down the action sheet and select "Add to Home Screen".
  3. 3 Tap Add in the top-right corner. AttendWise will appear on your home screen!
